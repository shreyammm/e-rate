How to interpret this

High overlap between different filer strings is a useful review candidate for a shared letter template, organization-provided language, or similar technical arguments. Exact duplicate families are indicated separately when recorded in the source index.

TF-IDF reflects terms present anywhere in documents, including quoted proposals and appendices. Similar submissions can disagree with one another. A high-scoring candidate requires original-source review, and a low score does not mean a lack of conceptual overlap.

Method: per-term TF capped at 15 by the public index; corpus-wide document frequency weighting; cosine normalization. Terms with document frequency under two and extremely common terms were omitted by the index builder. This is not semantic embedding or LLM inference.
