3. What the counting means

Filing counts do not equal distinct people, independently written arguments or population opinion. One organization may file many times and a campaign may produce many similar texts; we preserve source text hashes to aid sensitivity analyses. Organizational roles are provisional. Critically, a comment can quote an FCC question or an opposing argument without endorsing it: exact quote matching does not guarantee correct attribution. The model still may miss novel arguments.

Long source files can contain megabytes of quoted scientific material. The deadline-processing approach evaluates author-oriented windows while retaining the entire source archive; selected-window documents are separately flagged and should not be described as full-document LLM readings. Inspect every recorded machine claim and its original FCC evidence.
