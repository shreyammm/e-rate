Where the arguments may differ

These are reading leads from named filings, not corpus-wide conclusions about the industries.

What counts as screen time?

Edtech commenters distinguish instructional tools from recreational and social-media use, and challenge federal screen-time conditions on E-Rate. Compare the reasoning with schools' discussions of access, local decisions and student needs.

Curriculum Associates · SIIA

Which consultant safeguards?

Consultants discuss registration, personal-data disclosures, fees and compliance costs. These positions are not identical: review firm-level registration proposals separately from proposals about percentage-based compensation.

Funds For Learning · HWC Consultants · E-MPA

Who is actually a consultant?

Service-provider associations and sales-channel representatives have a different interest in the proposed definition than firms that administer E-Rate applications. Keep channel commissions, consultant fees and procurement conflicts distinct.

How are the arguments framed?

Explore specific recurring ideas within the edtech and consultant debate. This is a transparent, phrase-based grouping of machine-extracted arguments; it does not infer support or opposition.

Frames are matched against extracted action/reason text, rather than full-filing keyword searches. A single filing can match more than one frame. These are exploratory research leads and have not been fact-checked against the full context.

Reading the numbers correctly

Within stakeholder is the number of filings assigned to a group that contain an extracted argument, divided by all filings in that group, including filings with no detected theme.

Within argument is the number of filings raising an argument from a particular group, divided by all indexed filings with that argument.

Unique texts help identify repeated language. Filings, organizations, unique texts and supporters are different units; this analysis does not equate them.

Positions need separate verification. Machine-extracted claims can misidentify the speaker or reverse a policy direction. The NTIA listening-session compilation is not counted as the agency adopting third-party positions.

Checking missing arguments: The review queue assigns all 23 known ed-tech and consultant filing candidates and all 29 joint institutional filings to 18 priority themes, plus 924 sampled comparator assignments. Blank reviews deliberately include not detected by the model rows. They are not findings that an argument is absent. Confirm the actual speaker, complete source, requested action and supporting evidence before treating any extracted statement as endorsed.
