Interpretation and validation

This page deliberately avoids ranking organizations by how convincing they are or treating a trade association's filing as each member's stance. Counts should distinguish filings, distinct original texts, and actual reviewed individuals; the registry displays the former two. Automated identity labels remain candidates. The machine extracts at most five claims from a passage and long appendices can have partial semantic coverage, so a missing claim is not evidence the commenter has no view.
