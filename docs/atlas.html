04 · Competing considerations

Where should we investigate disagreement?

These are research questions, not prevalidated debates. They connect recurring issues in the corpus and give you one-click full-corpus queries to inspect whether—and how—actual filers disagree. No side is counted until its requested action is established from a source passage.

How the Atlas relates to the LLM analysis

Corpus-only: both unsupervised lexical clusters and 14 search-vocabulary families use the original extracted texts; the generated cluster descriptions in other data files are intentionally not displayed here. AI interpretation: GitHub Granite and Kaggle gpt-oss analyze individual filings for requested outcomes, arguments, and cited passages; they remain separate, unvalidated inference outputs until coverage and review are sufficient.

Regulatory crosswalk: the FCC proposal list remains available for deciding how any substantiated argument relates to a particular proposed or contemplated policy change. The 29 entries are an analyst-defined breakdown—not 29 equally weighted FCC proposals.
