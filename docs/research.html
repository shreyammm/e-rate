Research validity, not artificial precision

One filing is not one independent person. The docket is self-selected rather than a public opinion sample. Issue and phrase mentions are not automatically support or opposition. Role codes are provisional; duplicated comments, unusually long appendices, agency submissions, OCR errors, and quoted opposing arguments affect interpretation. When making a claim, open the FCC source record and distinguish the method used to derive it.
