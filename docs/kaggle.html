A completed filing is not always a fully read filing: 417 very long submissions had prioritized model windows. Another 316 readable filings contain no machine-extracted source-grounded positions. Methods & safeguards

Quotes were checked against original source character offsets. The model can still misinterpret a quote, reverse policy polarity, conflate separate positions, or miss evidence in long or OCR-damaged documents. Distinct-filings denominators are not unique individuals; thematic categories overlap. This research workspace never treats absent classifications as verified neutral positions.
