How to read these findings

The denominator is filings. Not individual supporters, distinct organizations, or representative public opinion. We also track identical source texts to expose repeated filings.

These are preliminary argument assignments. The original passages are real and source-linked, but the model may misinterpret a comment, miss a topic, or attribute a quotation to the filer.

Trade associations are kept separate from vendors. A joint submission remains a single filing. Consultants and clients are not automatically credited with each other's views.

Reporting isn't endorsement. NTIA's listening-session summary is excluded from the agency argument totals. Allegations of misuse are kept separate from routine calls for oversight.
