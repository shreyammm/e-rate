How to read these findings

The denominator is distinct metadata submitters. FCC filer names are normalized and repeat filings collapsed, including on argument charts. Incomplete or generic names are kept separate. An identical name may refer to different people and one person may use variant names, so these are estimates, not verified people or endorsements.

These are preliminary argument assignments. The original passages are real and source-linked, but the model may misinterpret a comment, miss a topic, or attribute a quotation to the filer.

Stakeholder categories remain distinct. A joint submission is one source record; consultant and client positions are not conflated. If a submitter crosses categories, their commenter–group assignments can exceed the corpus-wide unique-submitter total.

Reporting isn't endorsement. NTIA's listening-session summary is excluded from the agency argument totals. Allegations of misuse are kept separate from routine calls for oversight.
