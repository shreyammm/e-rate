NTIA reference and response research

Baseline: NTIA FCC filing 26110081266

This filing describes a listening session and reports third-party observations. Do not interpret every recommendation it summarizes as NTIA's own endorsed policy. Its existing machine stance labels are withheld from any agency-alignment assessment. Read its original text and establish a separately verified official-position baseline before declaring another comment contradictory.

An NTIA rebuttal requires (1) an accurately attributed outside claim, (2) the specific NTIA position or observation it relates to, and (3) checked independent evidence. A disagreement alone does not show factual error.

Method and what's not yet established

Identity evidenceSingle organization names in FCC submitter metadata are screened; person–institution pairs are flagged as possible affiliations, not confirmed representation.
Machine claimsKaggle's completed source-grounded extraction supplies candidate arguments, each linked to its original filing. Its model can reverse direction or incorrectly attribute voices.
Review firstHigh-risk claims should be checked against full originals. The NTIA listening-session summary is not treated as a list of official NTIA policy positions.
Independent fact checkingThe docket provides claims and counterclaims. External research, statutory interpretation, and NTIA-authorized positions require separate substantiation.
