    Interpretation rules

    Use counts to describe how often issue-related language occurs. Use within-category shares to avoid equating large organizations or categories with greater relative attention. Use duplicate weighting as a diagnostic against repeated identical texts. Never interpret a category's ratio as a likelihood of supporting a position or as a representative population estimate.

    All filings remain accessible via the FCC source link. The corpus-only analysis and model-based policy interpretation will be published as separate evidence layers.
