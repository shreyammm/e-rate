01 / The policy record

What should change—and why?

Each card represents a particular FCC question or proposed change. Evidence-backed position summaries will appear only after review; a text mention is not a vote.

02 / What the analysis measures

Evidence and denominators, not sentiment scores.

Requested regulatory action

Does a commenter want the FCC to adopt, reject, narrow, expand or modify an individual rule? Which alternatives do they propose?

Reasons and predicted effects

We distinguish costs, educational outcomes, children's safety, competition, privacy, free expression, statutory authority and implementation concerns.

Who speaks, and in what capacity?

A filing is not necessarily a distinct person or organization. We separate exact filer fields, verified stakeholder roles, repeated texts and template families.

Original evidence

Reviewed positions require a matching passage, source filing and a reproducible review record. OCR and non-comment documents receive separate treatment.

Public ECFS submissions are self-selected, not a public-opinion poll. Keyword matches are not positions. The currently reviewed sample must not be described as the views of all 3,231 filers.
