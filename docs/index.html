Methodology and boundaries

What a count represents

The unit for issue frequency is an ECFS filing ID. A single filing can address several issues. Exact phrase matching can find comments quoting arguments they reject. We have deliberately not converted these matches into automatic support/opposition percentages.

Commenter types

These are provisional principal capacities derived from metadata, the letter opening, and signatures across all 3,231 records. Personal-name-only fields are not proof of an unaffiliated citizen. Exact-normalized filer strings are not independently verified individuals or organizations. Individuals who list an employer and organizations represented by individuals may be miscategorized.

Positions and arguments

Organizational examples are initial source-linked qualitative analysis, with passages matched to extracted text, but not independently second-coded or sampled representatively. Reported agreements are limited to those checked filing/proposal pairs.

Repeated and very long filings

One exact filer-name field appears on 277 filings and accounts for 80.3% of extracted characters. Text-weighted analysis can drastically distort whose comments dominate. Repetition does not establish that filings are improper.

OCR and privacy

206 OCR candidate attachments were integrated into the snapshot, but source-text matching does not substitute for independent checking against the original scans. The index for this analytical table uses filing IDs and limited metadata. The separate full-text archive republishes the complete extracted ECFS filing texts (including OCR candidates) from the public source corpus; source documents can contain names and contact information.
