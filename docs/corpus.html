What this archive does—and does not—show


Each record is one FCC ECFS submission ID, not necessarily a unique person. Repeated and template submissions remain separate in the archive. Full text is shown as originally extracted into the research snapshot, including candidate OCR that may contain errors. One link-only record has no recovered comment text. Model-generated issue/stance labels belong on the separate LLM analysis page and do not change source text. Names or contact details in public FCC submissions may appear here.
