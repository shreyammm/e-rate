# FCC E-Rate named-commenter identity research — unpublished findings

**Research status:** Original-corpus audit and expanded source-method passes complete as of November 2, 2026. **NOT merged into main, NOT pushed to the live website.** This is not a claim that every commenter's profession is knowable.

## Baseline, units, and outcomes

The existing website placed **567 filings from 562 distinct normalized filer fields** in the *named person, capacity unstated* group. These are **not 562 anonymous people**. Most have names in the FCC filer metadata; their professional or stakeholder capacities were unestablished.

| Outcome | Filings | Distinct normalized filer fields |
|---|---:|---:|
| Initial named-person, capacity-unstated cohort | 567 | 562 |
| At least one recovered source-supported role/civic capacity | 97 | 96 |
| Individual filings still lacking a source-supported role | 470 | 468 represented in these filings |
| Distinct filers with **no** source-supported role in **any** cohort filing | — | **466** |
| Same filers with both role-supported and role-unstated filings | — | 2 |

These counts are *research-derived and provisional*. A recovered “library patron” or “resident” relationship is not proof of a person's employment. Conversely, a person whose profession is unstated is not *unidentified*.

## What new methods actually found

**Source-based phrase expansion:** 75 filings gained new self-declared roles from broadened but speaker-anchored rules, including students, parents, library patrons, teachers, library employees, school board members, residents, school IT professionals, and researchers. See [original 567-filing audit](results/summary.json).

**Entire original document scan:** independently read all original corpus shards (3,231 filings, manifest SHA-verified) and the 492 unresolved after the first pass, recovering 4 additional affiliations (2 library-community connections, 1 library employee, 1 school administrator). See [deep evidence](results/deep_full_document_evidence.json).

**Unscripted first-person phrase inventory and original-source inspection:** found 18 additional source-checked cases beyond the above, including an E-Rate service provider, a network supervisor at WSWHE BOCES, present and former library workers/directors, a teacher educator, a professional genealogist, senior citizen, parent, and two explicitly private-capacity comments. Each individual entry includes the original FCC filing ID, original-text regex, offset, and evidence note; raw personal details are not exported. The E-Rate service provider is **not** presumed to be an E-Rate consultant. See [18 source-specific findings](results/source_specific_identity_recovery.json).

**Whole-corpus same-text comparison:** looked for identical filings across the complete 3,231-file corpus; text-coordination is retained as its own evidence axis, **never** as proof of shared profession or organizational authority. The original cohort had 54 filings in short-/long-template clusters. See [full-document matching](results/deep_full_document_evidence.json) and [original review](results/review_queue.json).

**Independent model corroboration:** existing Kaggle labels often inferred sector from *topics*; Granite identity labels available for only 133 of 488 deep-residual filings were all unresolved or lacked verified occupation evidence; no validated automatic role promotion. A model's “advocacy group” label is not membership evidence. See [model and phrase review](results/model_and_phrase_review.json).

**Organizational letterhead and signatures:** identified 5 additional candidate institutional website domains printed in first-page source text, including KnowledgeWorks, AASA, Do No Harm, and Montgomery County Public Schools. Domain appearance alone **does not verify the signer worked for, or was authorized to represent, the institution**; it should prompt a targeted check. The same earlier corpus research also recovered explicit school-district authored comments in FCC filings 26110073398 (Pleasant Ridge Union School District) and 26110080616 (East Side Union High School District). See [institutional leads](results/institutional_letterhead_leads.json).

**FCC public ECFS metadata API:** discovered and used public metadata endpoint `https://publicapi.fcc.gov/ecfs/filing/<filing_id>?api_key=DEMO_KEY`. It returns `id_submission`, `filers`, `authors`, and `lawfirms`. After fixing schema parsing, **10** high-priority filings were confirmed through the live official source, but they revealed **no newly identified organizational affiliation** in those metadata fields. The next **15 calls returned HTTP 429** (demo-key quota). These calls are a **pilot**, not a complete 466-person live lookup. See [FCC metadata audit](results/fcc_public_metadata_crosscheck.json).

## What is left and what can be asserted

**466 distinct named filer identifiers have no reliable professional or stakeholder-capacity evidence from the methods completed.** They should remain `named_individual_capacity_not_stated` rather than be forced into parent, school staff, consultant, or advocacy-group buckets. The source scan found 193 of the remaining 470 filings are fewer than 500 characters, so the original comments frequently cannot sustain a precise occupational conclusion. The other 277 source comments could still benefit from human external-record corroboration if additional identifying information is available.

Across **all original 567 filings**, the following *overlapping topic cohorts* are useful even where profession is unstated: program continuity/eligibility **413**; filtering/privacy **72**; network/procurement **66**; screen time/parent choice **61**; education/learning **47**; library public access **25**; consultant oversight **7**; other/no coded topic **93**. These are **filing counts** and **do not imply the filers belong to any profession**.
