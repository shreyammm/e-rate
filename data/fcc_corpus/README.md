# Public FCC comment source corpus (OCR-augmented research snapshot)

Portable, gzipped JSONL shards sourced from `FCC_FULL_ANALYSIS_CORPUS.zip` supplied by the researcher. The archive contains 3,231 indexed FCC ECFS submissions and 3,230 available OCR-augmented or native comment texts.

This is the exact research snapshot, **not a live refresh** of the FCC docket. 206 OCR-derived attachments may contain unverified transcription errors. No source text is silently invented for the 1 record without a comment transcript.

Records include submission ID, FCC source URL, filer metadata, OCR flags and complete research text, with per-record SHA-256 and per-shard checksums in `manifest.json`. All included source normalized-text hashes were checked against the uploaded inventory.

Raw FCC filings may contain personal details. These files are published at the corpus owner's explicit request, and should not be interpreted as validated policy findings or a representative opinion survey.
